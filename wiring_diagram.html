✂️ Wiring Simplification: What to Connect vs. What to Skip

Device & Pin Status Where It Goes Why You Can Skip Or Bridge It
PCA9685 OE LEAVE FLOATING No wire connected Onboard pull-down resistor keeps PWM outputs active by default. Connecting to ESP32 is completely unnecessary!
MAX98357A GAIN LEAVE FLOATING No wire connected Internal pull-up automatically selects 9 dB gain (perfect loudness for 28mm mini speaker).
MAX98357A SD LEAVE FLOATING No wire connected Left floating = amp is always enabled and downmixes stereo I2S audio into (L+R)/2 mono.
INMP441 L/R LOCAL BRIDGE Solder blob to adjacent GND on Mic PCB Zero wires to ESP32! Bridging to the adjacent GND pin selects the Left audio channel right at the board.
Waveshare ST7789 BL GPIO 45 ESP32-S3 GPIO 45 (or local jumper to 3.3V) Firmware drives LCD_PIN_BL (GPIO 45) HIGH on boot (allows software backlight control; bridging to 3.3V also works for always-on).
PCA9685 Right Header LEAVE FLOATING No wire connected Only used for cascading a second PCA9685 board. Unused for 10 servos.
ESP32 GPIO 4-13, 15-18, 35-37 DO NOT WIRE Internal only Used internally on the Freenove CAM board for OV2640 camera bus and 8MB Octal PSRAM (note: GPIO 14 is free and used for PCA9685 SCL).

⚡ Assembly & Safety Rules

PCA9685 VCC MUST BE 3.3V: Never power PCA9685 logic from 5V! It will push 5V back onto GPIO 1 and 14 and fry the ESP32-S3 I2C pins.
ZERO SERVOS FIRST: Center CH0–9 to 90° (1500 µs) with horns detached before screwing into limbs to prevent stripped gears.
1000 µF BUFFER CAPACITOR: Install directly across PCA9685 V+ and GND screw terminals (negative stripe to GND) to absorb servo current surges.
CHARGING ISOLATION: Turn the CyberBrick power toggle OFF when charging via the 2S boost USB-C port so charger current goes purely into the battery.
